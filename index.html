Our Story

A Bakery Born from Passion

Harvest Bakery began with a simple dream — to bring handcrafted, fresh-baked happiness to every home in Patna. Nestled near Paras Hospital on Bailey Road, we've grown from a small neighbourhood bakery into one of Patna's most loved cake shops.

Every cake we bake is made with premium ingredients, artful decoration, and above all — love. From custom birthday cakes to elegant wedding tiers, from crispy patties to fluffy cupcakes, our menu is crafted to delight every palate.

With a 4.9★ rating and over 211 glowing reviews on Google, we're proud to be Patna's go-to destination for celebration cakes and everyday baked treats.
